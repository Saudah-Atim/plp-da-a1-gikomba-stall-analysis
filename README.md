# Project Summary

This project looks at Wanjiku's vegetable stall sales and stock for 14 days. It helps show how much the business made, which days had better sales, and which products need restocking.

### Top Three Findings

1 Total Sales: The stall made KES 46,450 in 14 days.

2 Weekend Sales: Weekend sales were 82.50% higher than weekday sales.

3 Stock: Tomatoes and Sukuma need restocking because their quantities are below the reorder levels.

#### Business Recommendations

1 Wanjiku needs to restock Sukuma and Tomatoes because their current quantities are below the reorder levels.

2 Wanjiku should concentrate more on weekends to increase sales because weekends have the highest sales.

3 Wanjiku needs to prepare more stock for weekends because they are the busiest selling days.
